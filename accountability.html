Accountability

Resolution of Accountability

Whereas it is the desire of the President of Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc and the Board of Directors to ensure that it maintains the highest levels of credibility, integrity, and accountability.

Be It Resolved that the Board of Directors approves the following resolution to be approved and to be followed by the President, Ministry Directors, Staff and all others who would be in the future become connected to Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc.

Statement of Policy

Because Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc operates on the basis of donations, special contributions, gifts, funding from general public, and profits from J.L.Designs, the organization has set high standards of receiving and distributing the finances intended for Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc.

  1. Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc uses all contributions, gifts, and donations designated for Shepherd's Gate Mission's to support the Shepherds Gate Missions Ministry, and all of its projects and programs caring for the needy.
  2. Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc guards against engaging in any form of activity that compromises our Christian ethics as it relates to finances.
  3. Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc keeps accurate books and records of all contributions, donations and gifts and offers tax receipts to those who request them after the end of each year.
  4. Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc does not and will not sell names of those on our e-mail or mailing lists to any other organization.

This Resolution of Accountability has been adopted by the Board of Directors for Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc on this 2nd day of January 2008.

President and CEO of Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc.
Dr. Julie Larsen
