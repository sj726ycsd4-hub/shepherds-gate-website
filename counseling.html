Addiction Recovery & Thought Management

Negative thinking is one of the primary contributors to stress, anxiety, and behavioral addictions. By bringing thoughts captive (2 Cor. 10:5) and establishing decision management, individuals can break free from destructive cycles.

1. Make a Choice: Seek God

Acknowledge the need for help and seek God wholeheartedly (Psalm 27:8) to break free from negative patterns.

2. Fix & Change: Renew Mind

Align thoughts with Scripture (Romans 12:2). Old patterns pass away as a new creation takes root (2 Cor. 5:17).

3. Accept Challenge: Deny Self

Deny unhealthy desires, take up your cross daily, and follow Christ (Luke 9:23).

4. Walk in the Spirit

Cultivate spiritual discipline through prayer, scripture meditation, and relying on God's strength (Galatians 5:16).
