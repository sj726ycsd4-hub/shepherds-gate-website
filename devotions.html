August 2024

“O LORD, You are the portion of my inheritance and my cup; You maintain my lot.” — Psalm 16:5 (NKJV)

Exposition: God had said to Aaron, when he gave him no special inheritance in Canaan, "I am thy Part and thine Inheritance among the children of Israel" (Numbers 18:20). David claims the same privilege. God is his "Portion," and he needs no other. A man's "cup" in Scripture is his lot or condition in life (Psalm 11:6, Psalm 23:5) — that which is given him to drink. David will have God only for his cup. "Thou maintainest my lot" — i.e. thou makest it firm and sure (comp. Psalm 30:6, "In my prosperity I said, I shall never be moved") (Pulpit Commentary).

January 2023 — Eight Means of Christian Growth

In the Knowing Jesus Study Bible (NIV), edited by Edward Hindson and Edward Dobson, eight means of growth for Christian character are highlighted:

  1. Faith (Trusting Faith): "Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see" (Hebrews 11:1).
  2. Goodness (Demonstrating Virtue): "For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light (for the fruit of the light consists in all goodness, righteousness and truth)" (Ephesians 5:8-9).
  3. Knowledge (Developing Insight): "But thanks be to God, who always leads us as captives in Christ's triumphal procession and uses us to spread the fragrance of the knowledge of him everywhere" (2 Corinthians 2:14).
  4. Self-Control (Holding Oneself in Check): "Be alert and of sober mind. Your enemy the devil prowls around like a roaring lion looking for someone to devour. Resist him, standing firm in the faith" (1 Peter 5:8-9).
  5. Perseverance (Persisting): "Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us throw off everything that hinders and the sin that so easily entangles. And let us run with perseverance the race marked out for us" (Hebrews 12:1).
  6. Godliness (Surrendering Completely): "For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come" (1 Timothy 4:8).
  7. Kindness (Affection for Others): "Therefore, as God's chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience" (Colossians 3:12).
  8. Love (Deep-Seated Concern): "Live a life of love, just as Christ loved us and gave himself up for us as a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God" (Ephesians 5:2).
