Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc. is a non-profit 501 (C) 3 Corporation ministering to the needs of people through SGM, it's projects, and programs. These projects and programs minister to children through adults and might include help with clothes, food, basic learning skills, and/or counseling to guide people to make better choices. All projects and programs are free of charge as SGM is a federal tax exempt public charity and gives a tax receipt for any monies or items donated. Anyone wanting to help people, please donate to Shepherd's Gate Mission's, Inc. and to email at contact@shepherdsgatemissions.com for the process until it is added to this site. SGM can give any material items to give to needy people and they can donate property, cars, and non-perishable food items; however, please contact SGM before you do this as certain items must be in usable condition.
