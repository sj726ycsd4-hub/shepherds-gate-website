Supporting Our Mission

501(c)(3) Tax-Exempt Public Charity

Shepherd's Gate Missions, Inc. is a federal tax-exempt public charity. All contributions, gifts, and donations are used directly to support SGM projects, programs, and community assistance initiatives caring for individuals and families in need.

Tax receipts are issued for all monetary donations and accepted material gifts. Material donations may include property, vehicles, clothing, and non-perishable food items.

Important Note on Material Gifts:

Please contact SGM via email prior to arranging delivery or property transfers, as donated items must be verified for usable condition.
